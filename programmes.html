What happens when you enquire?

Tell us which programme interests you, or briefly explain what you need help with. Our team will discuss suitable options, current dates, venues, costs and available places. An enquiry does not confirm a booking.

Please tell us about language, mobility or other access needs so we can discuss what would help.

Looking for the online EmpowerED Minds Circle?

The six-week community programme on this page is delivered through Spark & Spectrum CIC. Sofia’s paid monthly online Circle is a separate EmpowerED Minds by Sofia offer.

Bring a programme to your organisation

We welcome enquiries from schools, family hubs, women’s services and community organisations. Choose “Commission this programme” on a programme above, or tell us what your group needs.

We’ll discuss the audience, aims, group size, delivery format, access needs, costs and funding before agreeing the next steps.

For professionals & organisations

If you work with children, young people or families, there are several ways to connect with Spark & Spectrum. We welcome conversations with schools, SEND professionals, health services, community organisations and local partners.

Family, school and community partnerships

We work with families, schools and local services to help people find clearer routes to support. Contact us about collaboration, referrals or bringing support into your setting.

Before referring a family, make sure they agree to the referral. Please start with a general enquiry, without attaching sensitive family documents. We’ll explain what information is needed and how to share it.
